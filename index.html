01 — About

A data scientist who likes to look closely.

Muhammad Adeel is a Data Scientist and AI/ML Engineer with a strong focus on Computer Vision, Convolutional Neural Networks and Deep Learning. Most of his work sits at the intersection of vision and impact — fine-tuning CNN architectures to read medical scans, wiring together detection and OCR models into assistive tools, and building ensemble ML systems that hold up on real, messy data.

Machine learning is a genuine hobby as much as a profession — he regularly experiments with new architectures, cross-attention setups and fine-tuning techniques (LoRA, GroupKFold, SHAP) outside of formal projects, and is currently deep in a research build detecting Parkinson's disease from voice.
